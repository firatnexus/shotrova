Shotrova for Android: your screenshots, read on your phone, sorted into categories and found by what they say.

## What's new in 1.13.0

- **One app, made for Android.** Shotrova is now purely an Android app. The website version is gone, and so is everything that only existed for it: the installable web app, the browser service worker, the "add to home screen" steps and the "prepare for offline" button. Settings is shorter and only shows what applies to your phone.
- **All 12 recognition languages are part of the app from the first launch.** Turkish, English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Italian, Hindi, Arabic, Chinese, Japanese and Russian work offline the moment you pick them, with nothing to download or manage.
- **If an earlier version showed "language pack missing" on a screenshot,** that screenshot now reads "ready"; tap Retry and it's recognised with the built-in language.
- Your images, categories, rules and settings stay exactly as they are; updating over 1.12.x keeps everything.

## The app

- **Automatic capture (opt-in):** new screenshots are detected, copied into the app, titled and filed into a category, with a notification for each one.
- **Share → Shotrova** from the gallery or any app, the system photo picker, the clipboard, and drag and drop.
- **On-device OCR in 12 languages,** optionally combined with English. Images never leave the phone unless you turn on AI vision yourself.
- **11 categories** with confidence scores, a review queue and visible rules learned from your corrections.
- **Text search** over recognised text, title, notes, tags, category and file name.
- **Optional AI** with your own key: OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, OpenRouter or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint.
- **12 interface languages,** including a full right-to-left Arabic layout.
- **Private by design:** no server, no account, no analytics; ZIP/JSON backup and restore.

Install: download `shotrova-<version>.apk` below, open it and allow installing from this source if Android asks. Requires Android 8.0 or newer. You can check the file with `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
